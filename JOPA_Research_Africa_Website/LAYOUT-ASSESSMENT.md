# Website Layout Assessment & Redesign Direction

## Reference direction

The requested reference, Brand Metrics Africa, is positioned publicly as a client-focused research, brand development and communications consultancy. Its homepage could not be fully fetched by the automated review environment because the site returned a rate-limit response during assessment, so this redesign deliberately uses the *type of layout and visual hierarchy* expected from a modern research/consultancy website rather than copying its source code, wording or exact styling.

## JOPA Research Africa content retained

The redesign is based on the existing JOPA Research Africa positioning:

- Data-driven polling and insights
- Data collection
- Visualization
- Reporting
- Politics
- Financial sector
- Health
- Education
- Existing live voting/results platform

Sports has also been retained because it is listed in the current project README as one of the intended focus sectors.

## Main layout improvements

### 1. Stronger first impression
The previous homepage moved quickly from a short hero into sector cards. The new homepage uses a larger consultancy-style hero with a clear value proposition, two primary calls to action and a data-dashboard visual.

### 2. Services before sectors
Potential clients first need to understand *what JOPA Research Africa does*. The new services area separates polling, surveys, analysis, dashboards, reports and monitoring support.

### 3. More credibility through process
A four-step process explains how JOPA Research Africa moves from defining the question to collecting, analyzing and sharing insights.

### 4. Better separation between company website and polling platform
The homepage now works as the corporate/research landing page, while Vote, Results and Admin remain focused application pages.

### 5. Consistent visual system
All pages now share the same navigation, colors, typography, button style, cards and footer treatment.

### 6. Responsive structure
The navigation collapses on mobile and multi-column layouts adapt to tablets and phones.

## Visual direction used

- Deep navy for credibility and research/consulting tone
- Orange accent for calls to action and energy
- Cyan/blue for data and analytics cues
- Large editorial headings
- Structured whitespace
- Flat service cards and dark sector band
- Subtle data-grid background rather than stock imagery

## Recommended next content additions

When JOPA Research Africa has more business information available, the strongest next additions would be:

- About / company profile
- Real project or polling case studies
- Methodology and sampling approach
- Contact details and enquiry form
- Client/partner logos where permission exists
- Downloadable sample report
- Data privacy statement
- Terms for public polls
